The bRUSH Mechanics

Built to challenge your creativity, reward consistency, and show off your unique style.

15 Minutes. 1 Prompt.

Every day drops a single, unique prompt. Once you tap "Draw," the 15-minute timer starts. Choose from custom backgrounds (notebook, chalkboard, canvas) and sketch your masterpiece. Cancel, and you lose your chance for the day.

Medals & Leaderboards

Add friends and review their daily sketches. Award your limited Gold (100 pts), Silver (25 pts), and Bronze (10 pts) medals carefully. Every medal counts towards your weekly points to climb the competitive podium.

Expressive Avatars

Dive into the Avatar Studio to build your digital self. Mix and match bodies, shirts, eyes, hair, and backgrounds. Feeling weird? Switch to "Fun" mode for alien and creature customizations.

Animated Share Cards

Show off your creative consistency. bRUSH generates beautiful, animated video export cards highlighting your current streak, total drawings, earned medals, or your favorite showcase piece for social media.

Your Art. Your Data.

We believe your creativity belongs to you. Here is exactly how we handle your data in bRUSH:

Profile & Authentication

We use Firebase Authentication to securely store your login credentials. Your custom username, streak count, and detailed Avatar configurations (body, shirt, face, etc.) are stored in Firestore so your friends can see your digital identity.

Social & Leaderboard Data

bRUSH tracks your friend connections and the Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals you award and receive. This data is strictly used to calculate your weekly points and populate the competitive leaderboard.

Cloud Storage Limits

To protect your privacy and manage resources, cloud drawings stored on Firebase Storage are securely deleted every month. Your artwork remains safely stored on your local device permanently.

Firebase Service Data

Firebase Service Data is personal information that Google collects and generates during the provision and administration of the Firebase services, excluding Customer Data and Google Cloud Service Data. Google uses Firebase Service Data in accordance with our privacy policy and applicable terms.

Control your data:

You have control over how Google uses your Firebase Service Data. If you disable the option for Google to use this data for in-depth analysis of non-Firebase services, Firebase Service Data will still be used to make recommendations about and improve Firebase services, and to deliver and improve other services you request.
